# Undecoded vanilla entities that the vendored maps actually contain

Generated by `node tools/tas-fidelity/undecoded-entities.mjs vendor/celeste-game/Content/Maps`
(a substring scan of the 27 vanilla `.bin` files). Every name below is present in at least one
vanilla map and is **not** in `crates/celeste-physics/src/map.rs`'s decode table, so it arrives as
`EntityKind::Unknown` with no collider and no diagnostic - the same shape of gap that hid
`wallBooster`, `coreModeToggle` and `SummitBackgroundManager`.

Read the map counts as a *coverage* hint, not a priority order: what matters is whether the 202 TAS
touches the entity, which the fidelity report answers per segment. The list was built from the vanilla
classes that derive from `Solid` plus the `Trigger` subclasses, because those are the two families
that change player movement.

Two of them are modelled as of `753370f` + this commit and are kept out of the scan by
`decoded` in the script: `SummitBackgroundManager` (an `AscendManager`) and `plateau`/`bridgeFixed`
(static solids).
